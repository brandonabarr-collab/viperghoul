The ViperGhoul Museum

Archived Artifacts, Relics, and Corporate Casualties

Room 1: Corporate Casualties

A dimly lit hall dedicated to obsolete enterprises, featuring cracked signage, broken registers, and legally dubious permanent ban correspondence from the year 2000.

Room 2: 2000s Tech Graveyard

A glowing shrine to dial-up connection handshakes, low-poly CRT displays, tangled cords, and the dawn of digital distribution.

Room 3: The Lost VHS Vault

Endless floor-to-ceiling metal shelving units crammed with unlabeled video tape spines, glitchy static loops, and forgotten underground media.

Room 4: The 3D Artifact Showcase

Spotlighted obsidian pedestals and glass vitrines displaying custom-colored articulated prints, heavy arcade tokens, and independent physical crafts.
