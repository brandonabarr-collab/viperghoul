WING 01: THE CORPORATE CASUALTIES

You push open a heavy oak door and step into a dim, wood-paneled archive room that smells faintly of old paper and dust. Four distinct displays occupy the space, arranged quietly around the room.

North Wall: Blockbuster Video (1985 — 2013)

Mounted on a velvet backing sits an old blue-and-yellow membership card, resting below a plaque noting the exact moment management laughed Netflix out of a meeting room in 2000.

East Wall: Circuit City (1949 — 2009)

Beneath a flickering overhead light hangs a fragment of a massive rotating red neon sign, accompanied by a framed corporate memo detailing the brilliant strategy of firing all commission sales staff.

South Wall: RadioShack (1921 — 2015)

An old wooden parts drawer unit filled with mysterious resistors and capacitors, next to a faded annual battery club card and a blinking neon "You've Got Questions" sign that has finally gone dark.

West Wall: Toys "R" Us (1948 — 2018)

A solitary shelf holding a weathered plastic token of Geoffrey the Giraffe, displayed alongside a textbook on predatory leveraged buyout math that sealed the company's fate.
