WING 01: THE CORPORATE CASUALTIES

You step into a quiet, dimly lit room lined with heavy wooden panels. Four archival display cases rest in the stillness.

Exhibit Case 01-A // File #1985-2013

Blockbuster Video

Once reigning king of Friday night social destinations. Died of a terminal lack of imagination after laughing Netflix out of a meeting room in 2000. Preserved here as a warning against corporate arrogance.

Exhibit Case 01-B // File #1949-2009

Circuit City

Home of the massive rotating red neon sign and endless walls of car audio. Perished after deciding that laying off all experienced commission sales staff to save a quick buck was a masterclass in retail self-destruction.

Exhibit Case 01-C // File #1921-2015

RadioShack

You've got questions, they've got blank stares from cashiers who had no idea what a resistor was. Remembered fondly for the annual battery club cards and mystery parts drawers.

Exhibit Case 01-D // File #1948-2018

Toys "R" Us

Where every kid wandered warehouse aisles dreaming of absolute power. Succumbed to predatory leveraged buyout math, proving even Geoffrey the Giraffe couldn't outrun corporate financial engineering.
